<ansi fg="magenta-bold">Description:</ansi> The <ansi fg="command">stats</ansi> command provides you with detailed information about your character's current attributes, such as strength, dexterity, intelligence, and other vital stats. <ansi fg="magenta-bold">Note:</ansi> This command is useful for assessing your character's capabilities and planning skill development or combat strategies.
