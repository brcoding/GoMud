<ansi fg="magenta-bold">Section Heading:</ansi> <ansi fg="magenta">zone</ansi>

<ansi fg="black">The <ansi fg="command">zone</ansi> command is used to locate or get information about the current area or zone you are in. It helps identify the specific zone or region of the game world where your character is located.</ansi>

<ansi fg="black">Since the <ansi fg="command">zone</ansi> command is listed among the available commands, you can simply type:</ansi>

  <ansi fg="yellow">Usage: zone</ansi>

<ansi fg="black">This will typically display the name or details of your current zone, helping you orient yourself or plan your next move.</ansi>
